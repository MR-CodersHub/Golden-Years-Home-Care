Hospital Discharge Rapid Response

Mom Coming Home Tomorrow? We Can Have the House Ready.

Hospital and rehab case managers frequently mandate that home modifications must be completed before a patient is allowed to be discharged.

We interface directly with occupational therapists and case coordinators. We stock all standard 500-lb ADA grab bars, toilet risers, and modular threshold ramps on our service vehicles for immediate same-day deployment.

Call Discharge Line: (800) 555-0199

Discharge Prep Checklist

✓ 2x Shower Grab Bars (Vertical entry + horizontal seated support)
✓ ADA Toilet Riser with dual side support armrests
✓ Front doorway threshold beveled ramp for walker wheels
✓ Clear 36-inch continuous pathway from front door to bedroom
✓ Inspection verification letter emailed to hospital case worker
