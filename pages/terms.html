Terms & Conditions

The terms for safety assessments and home modifications. Last updated October 2026.

1. Free Assessments, Fixed Quotes

Safety consultations are free. Any paid work begins only after you approve a fixed written quote — the price we quote is the price you pay, with no hidden fees.

2. Certified Installations

Grab bars and handrails are anchored into structural studs or rated hollow-wall anchors, load-rated per ADA guidance, and backed by a 5-year workmanship warranty. Our $2M liability coverage and contractor license #TX-88492-H apply to every visit.

3. Scheduling and Cancellations

Reschedule or cancel any visit free of charge up to 24 hours beforehand by calling (800) 555-0199. Same-day emergency dispatches confirmed by phone are billable on arrival.

4. Portal Accounts

Family portal logins are for your household and designated caregivers only. You are responsible for keeping passwords private; tell us immediately about unauthorized access.

5. Questions

Contact us at care@goldenyearshomecare.com or visit 4820 Heritage Parkway, Suite 140, Plano, TX 75024.
