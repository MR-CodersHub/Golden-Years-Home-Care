Our Genesis Story

Born from Caring for Our Own Grandparents.

In 2014, when our founder’s 84-year-old grandmother suffered a preventable bathroom fall, the contractor hired to install grab bars drilled through water pipes and left drywall dust everywhere. He treated her home like a generic commercial job site.

We founded GoldenYears to ensure no senior ever experiences that indignity. We blended certified architectural accessibility with the warmth, patience, and respect of a loving family member.

National Standard of Excellence

Certified Aging-in-Place Specialists (CAPS)

The CAPS designation was developed by the National Association of Home Builders (NAHB) in collaboration with AARP.

It equips our master craftsmen with specialized knowledge in senior ergonomics, mobility biomechanics, universal design, and assistive hardware technology. We don't guess where a grab bar belongs—we calculate the exact trajectory of natural balance recovery.

✓ ADA 2010 Compliance: Strictly compliant mounting heights, grip diameters, and wall clearances.
✓ Universal Design Principles: Seamlessly functional for seniors today and adaptable for tomorrow.
✓ Annual Recertification: Rigorous continuing education in geriatric mobility equipment.
