Coverage Assistance

How to Get Reimbursed for Home Modifications

While traditional Medicare Part B does not typically pay for physical structural fixtures, there are multiple avenues for significant financial support — and we handle the paperwork with you.

01

Medicare Advantage (Part C)

Many private Advantage policies include supplemental safety benefits that cover bathroom grab bars and fall-prevention equipment.

Check Your Part C Plan
02

VA HISA Grants

The Veterans Administration provides Home Improvements and Structural Alterations grants up to $6,800 for service-connected veterans. We provide required line-item contractor bids.

Up to $6,800 for Veterans
03

Medical Tax Deductions

If modifications are recommended by a physician for a diagnosed medical condition, costs may qualify as deductible medical expenses (IRS Pub 502).

IRS Pub 502 Eligible
