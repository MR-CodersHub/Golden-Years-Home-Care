Coverage Assistance

How to Get Reimbursed for Home Modifications

While traditional Medicare Part B does not typically pay for physical structural fixtures, there are multiple avenues for significant financial support:

1. Medicare Advantage (Part C): Many private Advantage policies include supplemental safety benefits that cover bathroom grab bars and fall prevention equipment.
2. VA HISA Grants: The Veterans Administration provides Home Improvements and Structural Alterations grants up to $6,800 for service-connected veterans. We provide required line-item contractor bids.
3. Medical Expense Tax Deductions: If modifications are recommended by a physician for a diagnosed medical condition, costs may qualify as deductible medical expenses (IRS Pub 502).

Need Help with Itemized Insurance Invoices?

We provide formal contractor quotes with required diagnostic reimbursement codes (HCPCS) for your insurance provider or Long-Term Care insurance claims.
