Ask The Specialist

Q&A with Bob, Lead Senior Safety Inspector

Real questions submitted by adult children navigating parent care.

Q: “My father is 6'2" and my mother is 5'1". How do you mount grab bars that safely fit both of them?”

Bob Answers: “Great question! In couple households with height discrepancies, we never use a single horizontal bar. Instead, we install dual complementary bars—a 32-inch vertical entryway bar that spans multiple reach heights, paired with a 45-degree angled bar in the shower. This allows Helen to grip at 34 inches while Arthur grips comfortably at 38 inches.”

Q: “Will drilling into our fiberglass shower surround cause water leaks or crack the wall?”

Bob Answers: “Fiberglass shower stalls have a 1-to-2 inch hollow air gap behind them before reaching the wood studs. Drilling into fiberglass without solid backing will crush the surround. We use specialized hollow-wall fiberglass spreader brackets combined with structural stud anchors and marine-grade silicone. It creates a 100% watertight seal that never cracks the unit.”
