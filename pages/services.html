Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our certifications, warranties, and in-home procedures.

Will installing grab bars damage our decorative bathroom tile?

+

Never. Our technicians use water-cooled diamond-core drill bits that cut clean, microscopic holes through porcelain, marble, and ceramic tile without cracking or chipping. The mounting flanges include waterproof silicone O-rings that prevent moisture intrusion behind walls.

How do you know where to place the grab bars?

+

We do not use cookie-cutter placements. During our initial walkthrough, our CAPS specialist observes the senior’s natural movement patterns, height, arm reach, and dominant side. We physically mock up the bar positions and have your parent test them for maximum comfort before drilling.

How quickly can you install after a hospital discharge?

+

We reserve emergency priority slots every day for hospital and rehabilitation discharges. Call our Care Coordinator at (800) 555-0199 and we can often have critical grab bars, toilet risers, and entryway ramps installed within 24 to 48 hours.

Are your technicians insured and background checked?

+

Yes, 100%. Every GoldenYears craftsman undergoes federal criminal background screening, 10-panel drug testing, and carries $2,000,000 in general liability insurance and workers' compensation. They wear clean branded uniforms and photo identification at all times.
