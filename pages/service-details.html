Why Generic Anchors Fail & How We Fix It

When a senior slips, their body delivers an instantaneous dynamic shock load of up to 400 lbs onto whatever handle they grab.

Plastic drywall sleeves and toggle wings rip through 1/2” sheetrock in fractions of a second. GoldenYears uses Moen SecureMount patented stainless anchors and deep 3” solid wood lag bolts driven straight into 2x4 and 2x6 timber wall studs.

Water-Cooled Diamond Core Tile Drilling

We never use blunt hammer drills that shatter costly ceramic or porcelain. Our diamond bits cut smooth circular channels while water cooling prevents thermal stress fractures.
