Our Thoughtful Process

The "Gentle Care" Service Journey

From the first phone conversation to the final safety checklist, we keep seniors comfortable and family members fully in the loop.

1
Step 1 • Initial Discovery

Family Consultation & Medical Context

We speak with the family caregiver to understand the senior's mobility baseline, past fall events, walker or wheelchair usage, and upcoming discharge schedules.

2
Step 2 • Gentle Assessment

Dignity-First In-Home Safety Walkthrough

Our CAPS-certified craftsman visits the home wearing shoe covers. We measure grip heights, test floor slip resistance, and physically customize bar locations to your parent's exact reach.

3
Step 3 • Clean Installation

Commercial-Grade Mounting with Zero Mess

We drill precisely into wall studs, preserving ceramic tile and trim. HEPA dust vacuums run concurrently. Every bar is tested to 500 lbs before being declared safe.

4
Step 4 • Family Sign-Off

Family Portal Documentation & Warranty

Photos, inspection notes, and signed safety certs are automatically uploaded to your Family Dashboard. We coach the senior on proper grip techniques and follow up in 14 days.
